TENEBRA mainnet operational status

Decision: OPERATIONAL MAINNET LAUNCH CONFIRMED.
The deployed public node, website, persistence, backups, synchronization, restart recovery, block production, and confirmed transfer workflow are functional according to the operator evidence recorded for this release.

Verified release evidence

  • Production-named systemd service active and enabled at boot.

Scope

This addendum confirms deployed operational functionality and supersedes the launch posture of the dated pre-launch assessment for this operational scope. It does not claim perpetual uptime, market liquidity, absolute anonymity, immunity from undiscovered vulnerabilities, or that a short controlled throughput measurement equals competitive multi-miner Internet throughput.
