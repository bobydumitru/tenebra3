# TSC-1 founder treasury (draft)

The TSC-1 genesis allocation is **10,000,000 TNB** (10% of the maximum
100,000,000 TNB supply). It belongs to the founder and is intended to fund
project investment, promotion, and salaries. The remaining **90,000,000 TNB**
are reserved for proof-of-work mining.

The existence and size of this allocation must be publicly verifiable in the
genesis rules. The founder's real-world identity does not need to be encoded in
the blockchain. No third party has a protocol-level key to seize these coins.

Before a real genesis block can be fixed, the founder must generate a wallet
locally and provide only its **public receiving address**. Never put a private
key or recovery phrase in source code, a chat, or a repository. The current
demo creates disposable keys and does **not** allocate real coins to the founder.

No lockup or spending schedule has been specified. This document records the
intended allocation and uses; it does not establish a legal entity, investment
offering, or promise of returns.
